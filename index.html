Postgraduate Secretariat

Sekretariat Pascasiswazah Pusat Pengajian Sains Perubatan

"Cultivating Knowledge, Upholding Integrity, Achieving Excellence"

Vision

To become an authoritative, inclusive, and competitive School of Medical Sciences postgraduate student secretariat in supporting academic excellence, research, welfare, leadership, and professional development.

Mission

To serve as the official liaison between postgraduate students and the management of the School of Medical Sciences and the University, and to strengthen cooperation, communication, and networking within the postgraduate community.

Annual Activity Plan (2026)

February 2026

Engagement Session with Administration

Meeting Room, HPUSM

Strengthening cooperation between the secretariat and administration; coordinating academic and student welfare issues.

March 2026

Town Hall Meeting

Auditorium HPUSM

A two-way communication platform between faculty management (Dean/Deputy Dean) and postgraduate students.

Student Issues & Satisfaction Survey

Online Platform

Identifying student satisfaction levels and main issues to serve as a basis for improvement proposals.

June - July 2026

Inter-Departmental Sports Day

Promoting a healthy lifestyle and strengthening community ties between various departments.

August 2026

Community Service Program

Local Community / Schools

Fostering social responsibility among students and strengthening university relations with the community.
