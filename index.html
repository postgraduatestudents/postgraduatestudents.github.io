Sekretariat Pascasiswazah Pusat Pengajian Sains Perubatan

"Cultivating Knowledge, Upholding Integrity, Achieving Excellence"

Vision

To become an authoritative, inclusive, and competitive School of Medical Sciences postgraduate student secretariat in supporting academic excellence, research, welfare, leadership, and professional development.

Mission

To serve as the official liaison between postgraduate students and the management of the School of Medical Sciences and the University, and to strengthen cooperation, communication, and networking within the postgraduate community.
